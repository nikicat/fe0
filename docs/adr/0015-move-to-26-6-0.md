# 15. Move to Fe 26.6.0

- Date: 2026-11-11
- Status: accepted; its toolchain version is superseded by [0022](0022-move-to-26-7-0.md);
  supersedes [0009](0009-freeze-on-26-5-1.md) and the `Hasher` part of
  [0004](0004-literal-selectors-first.md)

## Context
[0009](0009-freeze-on-26-5-1.md) froze the toolchain on Fe 26.5.1. It allowed an upgrade only
for a blocking bug. Fe 26.6.0 came out on Nov 10. No bug forced the move. But on Nov 11, every
suite and every recorded spike value passed on 26.6.0 after a small port, and 26.6.0 is better:
- The whole compile shrinks from 56,102 to 52,367 runtime bytes at `-O s` (`spike-sizes/`).
  Compiling `corpus/early_return.fe` costs 11% less gas. The spike lexer costs 704,464 gas per
  KB at `-O 1`, down from 802,865. The output still uses no opcode newer than Cancun.
- It fixes seven 26.5.1 bugs: no stderr in native programs (argotorg/fe#1900), a silent
  native panic (#1883), integer literals cut short without an error, a dropped operand of an
  unused checked operation (#1853), a crash on a shift by a constant of 2⁶⁴ or more,
  same-named contracts in two modules (#1887), and keccak that did not lower natively (#1886).

## Decision
Build, test and submit on Fe 26.6.0, as the dev container pins it. The rest of
[0009](0009-freeze-on-26-5-1.md) still holds. We don't upgrade again in the window unless a
blocking bug needs it.

## Alternatives
- **Stay on 26.5.1:** no risk to the build, but a larger compiler and seven known bugs.

## Consequences
- Fe0 reserves `loop`, which 26.6.0 makes a keyword ([0001](0001-strict-subset.md)).
- `core::keccak` lowers natively, so `sol("…")` selectors need no `Hasher` effect and no
  software keccak. This record supersedes that part of [0004](0004-literal-selectors-first.md).
  The decision in 0004 to start with literal selectors still holds.
- The submission README (T0010) will explain the upgrade.
