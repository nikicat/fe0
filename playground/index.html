Fe0 playground

Fe0 is a subset of Fe, the smart contract language, with a compiler written in Fe. The compiler also runs on chain, as five contracts on Arbitrum One. This page sends your program to it: a compile is a free eth_call, and a deploy is a transaction from your wallet.

Deploy

      
Deploying sends deploy(src, args) to the compiler on chain,
        which compiles the program and creates the contract. args holds the values
        of the parameters of init, the contract's constructor, from the fields
        below. It costs gas on Arbitrum One, and the page asks your wallet to switch to that
        chain.

      

      

        
        
      

      

    


    

      
Call and send

      
      
A call is a free eth_call. A send is a transaction from your
        wallet. There is one form for each message the program's contract receives (its
        msg variants), guessed from the source. A wrong guess sends
        calldata that the contract does not expect, and it reverts.
